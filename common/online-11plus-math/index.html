Not just harder maths

Why 11+ Maths Is Different

11+ isn't school maths sped up — it tests reasoning and speed under real time pressure.

A child can be strong in class and still find the 11+ hard, because it asks for something school rarely does: solving unfamiliar problems quickly and accurately, against the clock. The gaps we find most often:

  • Arithmetic isn't automatic yet: hesitation on times tables and calculations costs precious time.
  • Reasoning question styles: multi-step and worded problems that don't look like the textbook.
  • Speed and accuracy together: getting it right isn't enough — it has to be right and fast.
  • Exam nerves: unfamiliar timed conditions throw a capable child.

The free assessment shows exactly which of these is the barrier, so preparation is targeted rather than generic worksheet grind.

GL Assessment, CEM & School-Specific Formats

Different grammar schools use different tests. We prepare your child for the one that counts.

11+ Maths appears as standalone maths or as numerical reasoning, and the style varies by school and test provider (such as GL Assessment and CEM-style papers). We tailor practice to the exact format and question style used by your child's target school — so nothing on exam day is a surprise.

When should my child start 11+ Maths preparation?

Year 4 or Year 5 is ideal, so fluency and reasoning build calmly over time. Focused Year 6 preparation still works well — the free assessment tells you honestly where your child stands and what's realistic.

How is 11+ Maths different from school maths?

It tests speed and reasoning under timed conditions, with unfamiliar, multi-step problems that don't look like classwork. A capable school student still needs specific 11+ practice.

Do you prepare for GL Assessment and CEM-style tests?

Yes. 11+ Maths appears as maths or numerical reasoning and the style varies by provider and school. We tailor practice to the format your target school uses.

Can you prepare my child for a specific grammar school?

How does the free assessment work?

It's a short online session where a tutor identifies exactly which 11+ Maths skills your child has gaps in, including speed and reasoning. There's no charge and no obligation.

Can online 11+ tuition work as well as in person?

Yes. Lessons are interactive and one-to-one or small group, so the tutor sees your child's working in real time and times practice properly — with none of the travel.
