Why we assess first

Most tuition guesses. We diagnose.

A child rarely struggles with a whole subject — they struggle with a few specific building blocks. Teach around those and progress stalls. The free assessment pinpoints the exact rung that's missing.

Once we can see the gap, every lesson has a target. Nothing is wasted on what your child already knows, and nothing important is skipped. That's the whole idea behind Improve Tuition: find the gap, then teach it.

Questions, answered

Before you book

Is the assessment really free?+

Yes — completely free and with no obligation. You'll receive a written summary and a learning plan whether or not you choose to book lessons with us.

How long does it take?+

Most assessments take around 30–45 minutes, depending on the year group and number of subjects. We'll agree a time that suits you.

Can it be done online?+

Yes. You can assess in person at our Batley centre or online. We support families across the UK and internationally.

Which years and subjects do you cover?+

Year 2 through to A-Level, in Maths, English and Science. If your child studies more than one subject, we can assess each.

Who carries out the assessment?+

Assessments and plans are set and reviewed by Gulamhusain Dabhad (QTS), and delivered by DBS-checked subject specialists.

Is there any pressure to sign up afterwards?+

None. The report and plan are yours to keep. If another route would help your child more, we'll say so honestly.
