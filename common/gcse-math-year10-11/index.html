The real problem

Why Students Struggle With GCSE Maths

Most students aren't "bad at Maths" — an earlier topic was never fully understood.

When a foundation topic is shaky, it quietly breaks the harder GCSE questions built on top of it. Revising on top of a hidden gap rarely works, which is why so many students feel stuck no matter how much they revise. The four causes we see most often:

  • Missed foundations: a weak grasp of fractions, ratio, percentages or basic algebra breaks later topics.
  • Rushed teaching: a full class moves on before every student is genuinely ready.
  • Lost confidence: "I'm just bad at Maths" becomes a barrier of its own.
  • No diagnosis: nobody has pinpointed the specific gap, so effort is spread thin across everything.

Our free assessment finds the exact barrier — whether that's a grade 3 to 5 journey on Foundation or a grade 6 to 8/9 push on Higher — so every lesson afterwards is targeted, not generic. Once the gap is clear, progress usually comes faster than parents expect.

Questions parents ask

Online GCSE Maths Tutor FAQs

Is online GCSE Maths tuition effective?

Yes. Lessons are one-to-one or small group and interactive, so the tutor sees the student's working in real time and targets the exact gap. Many families find the focus and convenience help progress rather than hinder it.

How does the free assessment work?

It's a short online session where a tutor identifies exactly which GCSE Maths topics your child has gaps in. There's no charge and no obligation — you get a clear picture before deciding on any paid tuition.

Can a tutor help my child move from grade 3 to grade 5?

Often, yes — it's one of the most common journeys we support. The assessment finds the specific topics blocking the grade, and lessons target them alongside exam technique.

Should my child take Foundation or Higher?

The assessment gives you an evidence-based answer rather than a guess. Foundation secures a strong pass (up to grade 5); Higher stretches to grades 7–9. We teach both and review the choice as your child progresses.

Which exam boards do you cover?

We support the major GCSE Maths boards including AQA, Edexcel and OCR, and tailor past-paper practice to your child's board.

Can you help before the mocks?

Yes. If mocks are close we prioritise the highest-impact gaps and the exam technique most likely to move the grade in the time available.

How soon should my child start?

The earlier the better — Year 10 is ideal for fixing gaps without exam-year pressure — but focused Year 11 support still makes a real difference. The best first step is the free assessment.
