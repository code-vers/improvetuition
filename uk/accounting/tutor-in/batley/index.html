02 / What May Be Happening

Same low mark. Different cause.

Two students can get the same Accounting grade for completely different reasons.

  • One understands the theory but makes a single entry slip that breaks the whole set of accounts.
  • Another gets every figure right but never interprets or evaluates it, so the written marks stay out of reach.
  • A third knows the method but loses marks to layout and accuracy on every statement.

More past papers won’t fix a problem we haven’t named yet.

06 / Questions

The questions students & parents ask most.

Is the Accounting assessment really free?

Yes. There is no payment to book the assessment and no obligation to take tuition afterwards. We use it to find your A Level Accounting gaps and explain the clearest next step before any paid lessons begin.

My child can do the numbers but can't get past a B. What's going on?

This is the most common A Level Accounting pattern we see. The grade ceiling is rarely the calculations — it's the higher-mark skills: interpreting ratios and statements, justifying recommendations, and writing developed analysis and evaluation on the extended questions, alongside the accuracy and layout that protect marks across a long paper. The assessment pinpoints which of these is costing the marks.

Which exam board do you cover?

AQA is the main A Level Accounting specification, and tuition is matched to your child's exam board — including its specific question styles, statement formats and mark schemes. Tell us the board at the assessment and we'll work to it.

Do you cover both financial and management accounting?

Yes — the full A Level: financial accounting (double-entry through to final accounts for sole traders, partnerships and companies, plus ratio analysis) and management accounting (costing, budgeting, variances and investment appraisal), along with the written analysis and evaluation the papers reward most heavily.

What happens after the assessment?

We explain what the student can do securely, where the Accounting gaps are, and what needs to be fixed first — whether that's a specific topic, double-entry accuracy, statement layout, or evaluation technique. If tuition is suitable, we recommend the clearest next step and how lessons would work.

How do lessons work?

Lessons start from the gaps found in the assessment, not a generic worksheet pack. A qualified teacher guides the plan and our DBS-checked Accounting tutors deliver focused one-to-one lessons, working on real exam questions and mark schemes. Pace is set by the student's understanding, not a timetable.

How much does A Level Accounting tuition cost?
