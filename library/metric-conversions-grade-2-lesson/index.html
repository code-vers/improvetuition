Metric Conversions

Convert between units. Bigger unit = smaller number. Smaller unit = bigger number.

The six facts you need

Every metric conversion uses one of these. You don't need to remember anything else.

1 · Length10 mm = 1 cm · 100 cm = 1 m · 1000 m = 1 km.
2 · Mass1000 g = 1 kg.
3 · Capacity1000 ml = 1 l.
4 · Going to bigger unitsDIVIDE. Smaller-to-bigger unit means the number gets smaller.
5 · Going to smaller unitsMULTIPLY. Bigger-to-smaller unit means the number gets bigger.
6 · Multi-stepCombine: mm to m in one go = ÷ 1000. m to mm = × 1000.

Worked example 1 - mm to cm

Convert 250 mm to cm.

  1. Read it. Convert 250 mm to cm.
  2. The fact. 10 mm = 1 cm. mm to cm (bigger unit): ÷ 10.
  3. 250 ÷ 10 = 25.
  4. So 250 mm = 25 cm.
Answer: 25 cm
Watch outGoing to a BIGGER unit means the NUMBER gets smaller. Divide, don’t multiply.

Worked example 2 - cm to mm

Convert 3.5 cm to mm.

  1. Read it. Convert 3.5 cm to mm.
  2. The fact. 1 cm = 10 mm. cm to mm (smaller unit): × 10.
  3. 3.5 × 10 = 35.
  4. So 3.5 cm = 35 mm.
Answer: 35 mm
Watch outGoing to a SMALLER unit means the NUMBER gets bigger. Multiply.

Worked example 3 - cm to m

Convert 450 cm to m.

  1. Read it. Convert 450 cm to m.
  2. The fact. 100 cm = 1 m. cm to m: ÷ 100.
  3. 450 ÷ 100 = 4.5.
  4. So 450 cm = 4.5 m.
Answer: 4.5 m
Watch outDivide by 100, not 10. Different pair of units, different power.

Worked example 4 - m to cm

Convert 2.7 m to cm.

  1. Read it. Convert 2.7 m to cm.
  2. The fact. 1 m = 100 cm. m to cm: × 100.
  3. 2.7 × 100 = 270.
  4. So 2.7 m = 270 cm.
Answer: 270 cm
Watch outTo multiply by 100, move the decimal point 2 places to the right.

Worked example 5 - m to km

Convert 3000 m to km.

  1. Read it. Convert 3000 m to km.
  2. The fact. 1000 m = 1 km. m to km: ÷ 1000.
  3. 3000 ÷ 1000 = 3.
  4. So 3000 m = 3 km.
Answer: 3 km
Watch outTo divide by 1000, move the decimal point 3 places to the left.

Worked example 6 - g to kg

Convert 2500 g to kg.

  1. Read it. Convert 2500 g to kg.
  2. The fact. 1000 g = 1 kg. g to kg: ÷ 1000.
  3. 2500 ÷ 1000 = 2.5.
  4. So 2500 g = 2.5 kg.
Answer: 2.5 kg
Watch outSame rule as m → km: divide by 1000.

Worked example 7 - l to ml

Convert 1.75 l to ml.

  1. Read it. Convert 1.75 l to ml.
  2. The fact. 1 l = 1000 ml. l to ml: × 1000.
  3. 1.75 × 1000 = 1750.
  4. So 1.75 l = 1750 ml.
Answer: 1750 ml
Watch outSame rule as kg → g: multiply by 1000.

Worked example 8 - multi-step: mm to m

Convert 4500 mm to m.

  1. Read it. Convert 4500 mm to m.
  2. The fact. mm to m in one go: ÷ 1000 (because 10 × 100 = 1000).
  3. 4500 ÷ 1000 = 4.5.
  4. Or step-by-step: 4500 ÷ 10 = 450 cm; 450 ÷ 100 = 4.5 m.
Answer: 4.5 m
Watch outTwo-step conversion. Either divide once by 1000 or divide twice (by 10, then 100).
