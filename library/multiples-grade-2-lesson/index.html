Is 24 a multiple of 6?

(1 mark)

  2. A multiple of 6 means a number you get by multiplying 6 by a whole number.

  4. 4 is a whole number with no remainder.
  5. So 24 IS a multiple of 6, because 4 × 6 = 24.

Q3.
Is 38 a multiple of 4?
Your answer:
(1 mark)
  1. We need to check if 38 is a multiple of 4.
  2. Try dividing: 38 ÷ 4 = 9.5.
  3. 9.5 is NOT a whole number (it has a decimal).
  4. So 38 is NOT a multiple of 4.
  5. Check using the last-two-digits rule for ÷4: the last two digits of 38 are 38 itself.
  6. Is 38 in the 4 times table? 4 × 9 = 36, 4 × 10 = 40. 38 is between two multiples of 4, but isn’t one. ✗
  7. Watch out: 38 is even, but even numbers aren’t always multiples of 4. Multiples of 4 are 4, 8, 12, 16, 20, 24, 28, 32, 36, 40, …
Q4.
Is 100 a multiple of 7?
Your answer:
(1 mark)

  2. Try dividing: 100 ÷ 7 = 14.28...
  3. 14.28... is NOT a whole number.
  4. So 100 is NOT a multiple of 7.
