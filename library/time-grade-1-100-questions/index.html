Section — Elapsed minutes with a time bar
How many minutes between two times
Q61.From 09:00 to 09:45, how many minutes have passed?
(1 mark)
  1. Read it. Find total minutes from 09:00 to 09:45.
  2. The fact. Break at the hour if needed.
  3. The numbers. Start: 540 minutes since midnight. End: 585.
  4. Do the maths. 585 − 540 = 45 minutes.
  5. Answer. 45
Answer: 45
Watch outWhen crossing an hour, count up to the hour then add the rest — do not just subtract minutes.
